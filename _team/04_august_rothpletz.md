August Rothpletz serves as the chief marketing officer and a member of the board of directors for Komolion Human Development Fund. August discovered his love for the people of Kenya and his passion for sharing their stories on his first trip to Kenya in 2023, which was soon followed up by two more visits in 2025 and 2026. He believes that education is the best path towards empowerment and that cross-cultural interaction forms better global citizens, and he seeks for the Komolion Fund's marketing efforts to be evidence of this. Before joining the Komolion Fund board, August discovered his knack for marketing through running the Baylor University Men's Choir's social media, which grew under his leadership by over 115,000 followers through videos totaling over 80 million views. August is also currently a software engineer at EControls in San Antonio, Texas.
