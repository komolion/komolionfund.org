Robin McAlister serves as the secretary of the Komolion Human Development Board of directors. Robin has been interested in international development since her first overseas study trips in college. She visited Kenya with Randall and Brenda Bradley for the first time in 2016 with her mother, Mary Kay Parrish, who was a colleague of Randall's at Oklahoma Baptist University. In 2025, she returned to Kenya with Randall and the Komolion Fund and was able to introduce her husband and children to the country's amazing scenery and people. She earned a bachelor’s degree in English from Oklahoma Baptist University and a master’s degree in public policy from Duke University, previously serving in a variety of roles in state government. Currently a marketing consultant for McAlister Construction, a real estate development firm founded by her husband in Oklahoma City, Robin also is mother and chauffeur to two active middle school-aged children.
